# Hardware baseline

## Retroid Pocket 5

- Qualcomm Snapdragon 865 / SM8250
- Adreno 650
- 8 GB RAM
- Android 13
- Firmware: V02.05.001.01
- Build: RP5 V1.0.0.189

## Emulator baseline

- Eden Legacy v0.2.0-rc1

## Driver references

### R9v2
Mesa Turnip 24.3.0 Revision 9v2.

Project-owner observation: currently the more stable reference.

### T19
MrPurple T19-toasted, Mesa 25.1.0-devel.

Project-owner observation: feels faster and appears to show more detail, while being less stable than R9v2.

These are device-specific observations and are not universal benchmark claims.
